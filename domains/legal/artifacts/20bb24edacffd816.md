## Question

I ended my month-to-month tenancy with 30 days' written notice, effective the 21st, and paid pro-rated rent up to that date. I've already moved out and returned the keys, with the unit in good condition. The landlord has re-let the unit to a new tenant from the 18th. Am I entitled to a refund for the 18th to the 21st, and if so, at my rate or the new tenant's lower rate? My lease doesn't address this.

## Answer

Yes, you have a good claim to a refund. In California a landlord cannot collect rent from two tenants for the same days in the same unit. Once he re-rented the unit starting on the 18th, your obligation for the 18th through the 21st was effectively cut off, and any rent you prepaid for those days is owed back to you. As to the amount, the safer expectation is a refund equal to what the new tenant actually paid for those days, since the landlord may keep the shortfall if he re-rented for less. However, you can reasonably argue for the full amount at your rate, because by taking the keys and placing a new tenant he accepted your early surrender. Request the refund in writing, ask that it be included with your security deposit accounting, which is due within twenty-one days of move-out, and if he refuses, small claims court is the usual remedy.
