## Question

I live in a shared house where each bedroom has a lock. I keep finding keys in communal areas, such as at the bottom of cupboards, that look like they fit the bedroom doors. Would it be legal to try them in other rooms' doors? I want to know if the landlord is leaving tenants' keys lying around, since then a key to my room could be too. I'd normally ask, but several rooms are vacant and other tenants are away.

## Answer

No it is not legal; it is trespassing.

the common law definition of trespassing is to enter someone's land or property without their permission. By locking the door they have denied permission to everyone **except** those to whom **they** have given a key. If someone finds a key that happens to fit the lock, there is no longer a physical restriction but as they do not have permission, the legal restriction is still in force.
