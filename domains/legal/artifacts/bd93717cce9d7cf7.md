## Question

I lent someone my car and it came back in bad condition: the horn and reverse lights don't work, the windows are cracked, and more. Can I legally make them fix it?

## Answer

Yes, in most cases you can. When you lend someone your car for free, they must look after it with reasonable care and return it in the condition they received it, allowing only for ordinary wear and tear. A broken horn, failed reverse lights and cracked windows go well beyond normal use, so the borrower is generally liable for damage caused by their carelessness or misuse, unless they can show it happened through no fault of theirs. You cannot force them to do the repairs personally, but you can claim the reasonable cost of fixing the car. Get two or three written quotes, then send a written demand giving a deadline for payment. If they refuse, you can sue in the Small Claims Court for smaller amounts without a lawyer, or in the Magistrate's Court for larger claims. Keep photos, messages and any witness details as evidence.
