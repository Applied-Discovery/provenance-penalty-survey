## Question

My gym overcharged me and refused to correct it after I asked about three times, so I cancelled my membership. Can I sue them?

## Answer

Yes. If the overcharge is modest, small claims court in New York is built for exactly this kind of dispute: the filing fee is low, you do not need a lawyer, and you can recover the overpaid amount plus court costs. Before filing, send the gym a short written demand by mail or email stating the amount owed and a deadline to refund it; this often resolves things and creates a useful record. Gather your membership contract, bank or card statements showing each charge, and any messages from your earlier requests. You can also dispute the charges with your card issuer or bank if they are recent, and file a complaint with the New York Attorney General, which enforces specific consumer protections for health club memberships. If the gym violated those rules, you may be entitled to additional damages beyond the refund.
