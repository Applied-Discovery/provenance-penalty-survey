Sleep now, my darling, the lamp's burning low,
Sleep now, my darling, the wind's at the door,
Sleep now, and dream, for there's nowhere to go,
Hush, hush, my little one, hush, nothing more.
Sleep now, my darling, the clock ticks along,
Sleep now, my darling, the night is so long,
Hush, hush, my little one, hush, nothing more,
And I'll rock you and wait, my sweet, my sweet, my own sweet one, till the step's at the door.
