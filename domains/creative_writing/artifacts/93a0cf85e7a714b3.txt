What is it in your smile that undoes the dark?
The kitchen fills as if a shutter's thrown,
And every dull thing wakes to catch the spark,
The kettle, the window, the chipped blue bowl.
I have seen dawn come slow across the fields
And thought it lovely, but it never knew my name.
Your smile does. It turns, and I am found,
Warm in a light that asks for nothing back.
