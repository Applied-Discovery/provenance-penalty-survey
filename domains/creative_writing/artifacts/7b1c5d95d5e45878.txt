A dewdrop is a lantern hung
  Too heavy for its thread;
A clover is a chapel roof
  With green above my head.

A pebble is a mountain range
  I climb for half a day;
A puddle is an ocean where
  The leaf-boats sail away.

A footstep is a thunderclap,
  A breath is weather blown;
And I am something no one sees,
  A kingdom of my own.
