# Income tax should be abolished and replaced with taxes on what people buy.

Income tax punishes the very thing a healthy economy needs most: work. Every extra hour earned is clipped before it reaches the pay packet, through a system so tangled that ordinary people pay accountants simply to obey it. A tax on spending is simpler and fairer. Everyone contributes when they buy, including tourists and those who hide their earnings from the taxman, so the base is broader and evasion harder. Necessities such as food and children's clothing can be zero-rated, protecting poorer households, while a new car or a yacht attracts the full charge. Saving and investment would no longer be penalised, and the incentive to earn more would return.
