# Homework should be banned.

Homework should be banned because it takes the hours children need for rest, play and family life and gives little back. Studies of primary pupils find almost no link between homework and attainment, yet a seven-year-old still comes home to worksheets after a full school day. Homework also widens the gap between children: some have a quiet desk and parents who can help, others share a crowded flat with parents out at work. Teachers spend hours setting and marking it that would be better used planning lessons. If a subject matters, teach it properly in class. Let evenings belong to families.
