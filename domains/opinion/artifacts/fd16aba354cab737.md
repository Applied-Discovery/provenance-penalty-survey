# Speed cameras should be used to enforce speed limits.

Speed limits only work if drivers expect to be caught breaking them, and no police force can watch every road all day. Cameras can. They do not tire, take breaks or let a driver off because he seemed sorry, and they apply the same rule to everyone. Where they have been installed, at accident blackspots and outside schools, crashes and casualties have fallen, often sharply. Critics call them a revenue grab, yet the fine is entirely avoidable: keep to the limit and you pay nothing. A speed limit that is never enforced is merely a suggestion, and suggestions do not protect pedestrians, cyclists or children walking to school.
