# Government space missions should be contracted out to private companies.

Governments are poor at building rockets and good at setting goals. Contracting missions out lets each side do what it does best. Private firms compete on price and schedule, and the results are already visible: commercial cargo and crew flights to the space station have cost a fraction of what the old shuttle programme did, and reusable boosters have made launches routine. A state agency faces no such pressure, because overruns are simply absorbed by the taxpayer. The agency should still choose the destinations, write the safety rules and own the science. But the hardware and the launches should go to whoever can deliver them most cheaply and reliably.
