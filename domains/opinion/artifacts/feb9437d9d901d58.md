# Advertising does more harm than good.

Advertising exists to make people want things they did not previously want. That is its whole purpose, and it is a strange thing to celebrate. Billions are spent each year telling us we are too fat, too plain, too behind the times, and that the remedy is for sale. The result is not better-informed shoppers but a steady drip of dissatisfaction, aimed increasingly at children who cannot tell a sales pitch from a story.

Advertising also funds the tracking that follows us across the internet, pushes junk food and gambling onto the people least able to resist them, and inflates the price of everything it sells. If a product is genuinely good, word gets round. The rest is noise we pay for.
