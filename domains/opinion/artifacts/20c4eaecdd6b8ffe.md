# Oil and gas exploration should not be allowed in the Antarctic.

Antarctica is the last continent without a resource industry, and it should stay that way. Its ice holds most of the planet's fresh water, its waters feed the krill on which whales, penguins and seals depend, and its remoteness makes any accident almost impossible to contain. A spill in the Southern Ocean could not be cleaned up in a place where storms last for weeks and help is thousands of miles away.

The world already has more known oil and gas than it can safely burn. Drilling in the one region nations agreed to preserve for peace and science would be a step backwards on climate and a betrayal of an agreement that has held for decades.
