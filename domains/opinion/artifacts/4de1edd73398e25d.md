# School uniforms should not be mandatory.

Uniforms are sold as a cure for bullying and inequality, yet children still find ways to rank each other by trainers, phones and haircuts. What uniforms reliably do is add a cost. Blazers, branded jumpers and regulation shoes can only be bought from approved suppliers, and families on tight budgets pay far more than they would for ordinary clothes.

Compulsory uniforms also teach the wrong lesson. Schools claim to prepare pupils for adult life, but few workplaces dictate a single outfit. Letting young people choose sensible clothes within a dress code builds judgement and self-respect. A voluntary uniform, available to those who want it, gives families the choice without the penalty.
