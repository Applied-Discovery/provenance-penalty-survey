# Biofuels are the best way to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

Biofuels cut emissions today, not in some distant future. The carbon released when they burn was drawn from the air by the crops that made them only months earlier, so the cycle is close to neutral. Better still, they slot into the vehicles, pipelines and refineries we already have. Brazil has run its cars on sugarcane ethanol for decades, and airlines are already flying on fuel made from used cooking oil and farm waste.

Electric cars need new grids, new mines and years of investment; hydrogen remains a promise. Ships and aircraft, which produce a large share of transport emissions, cannot run on batteries at all. Biofuels can power them now, while creating rural jobs and turning waste into value.
