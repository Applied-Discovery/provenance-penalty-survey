# Fines should be set relative to the offender's income.

A fixed fine is not a fixed punishment. A £100 speeding ticket is a real deterrent to a cleaner on minimum wage, but pocket change to a banker, who can treat it as the price of driving as he pleases. The law says both committed the same offence, yet only one of them is actually punished. Finland and Switzerland already scale fines to daily earnings, and their roads are no more lawless for it. If the point of a fine is to make people think twice, it has to sting everyone roughly equally, and that means tying it to what they earn.
