# Summer holidays should be shortened.

Six weeks away from the classroom is longer than most children need and longer than most families can comfortably absorb. Pupils forget a measurable amount of what they learnt, and teachers spend September re-teaching it, which is time lost from the year. The loss falls hardest on children whose parents cannot afford tutors, camps or trips, so the gap between rich and poor widens every August.

Working parents face weeks of childcare they must patch together with grandparents, holiday clubs and unpaid leave. A four-week break, with the spare days spread across the year as longer half-terms, would keep the rest that children genuinely need while giving families and schools a calmer, more even rhythm.
